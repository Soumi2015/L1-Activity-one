Food

Food is any substance consumed to provide nutritional support and energy for the body. Made of essential nutrients like carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, and minerals, it fuels daily metabolism, supports physical growth, and protects overall health.

Chinese Cuisine

Chinese cuisine is one of the world's oldest and most diverse culinary traditions. Characterized by profound regional variation and an emphasis on texture, aroma, and color, the diet traditionally balances grains, proteins—notably tofu and pork—and an abundant array of fresh vegetables. Ginger, garlic, and chili are essential building blocks for most savory dishes. Rice is the primary staple in the warm, humid South, while wheat-based products like noodles, steamed buns, and dumplings dominate the colder, drier North. The flavor base is often enriched with soy sauce, rice vinegar, Shaoxing rice wine, sesame oil, and fermented bean pastes. Traditional Chinese cuisine is largely dairy-free. Calcium and protein needs are met instead with dairy alternatives like soy milk and tofu.

Peking Duck

Peking Duck is a celebrated Chinese delicacy originating from Beijing, famed for its incredibly thin, crispy skin and succulent meat. Historically an imperial dish, it is traditionally served with thin pancakes, sweet bean or hoisin sauce, scallions, and cucumbers, assembled into small, flavorful wraps.

Mapo Tofu

Mapo Tofu is a celebrated Sichuan comfort dish featuring tender, silken tofu and savory minced meat simmered in a spicy, tongue-numbing chili sauce. Originating in the 1860s in Chengdu, China, it is the ultimate expression of málà—the iconic combination of spicy heat and numbing flavor.

Dim Sum

Dim sum is a traditional Chinese dining experience featuring a vast array of bite-sized, small-plate dishes, mostly associated with Cantonese cuisine. Served in bamboo steamers or on small plates, it is traditionally enjoyed in the morning or early afternoon alongside bottomless pots of Chinese tea (yum cha).

Hot Pot

Hot pot is an interactive, communal dining experience where diners cook raw ingredients—like thinly sliced meats, vegetables, and seafood—in a pot of simmering broth placed in the center of the table. It is a highly customizable, fun, and social meal popular across East and Southeast Asia.
