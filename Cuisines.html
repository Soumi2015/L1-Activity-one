Cuisines Of Different Places

Let's learn about some cuisines!

1. Samosa

* A samosa is a globally popular fried or baked pastry with a savoury filling, most commonly made of spiced potatoes, onions, and peas. While quintessentially Indian today, it actually originated in the Middle East and Central Asia around the 10th century as a meat-filled snack for travellers.

2. Momo

* Momos are popular Tibetan and Nepali dumplings, now a staple street food in India and Bhutan, featuring savory fillings like minced meat, vegetables, or cheese wrapped in thin dough. They are typically steamed, but also served fried or in soups, usually accompanied by spicy tomato-based chutneys.

3. Idli

* Idli is a popular, healthy South Indian breakfast staple consisting of soft, fluffy steamed cakes. Made from a fermented batter of rice and black gram (urad dal), it is low in calories, high in protein, and naturally gluten-free. It is commonly served warm with coconut chutney and lentil-based vegetable sambar.

4. Dosa

* A dosa is a popular South Indian savory crepe made from a fermented batter of rice and black lentils (urad dal). It is thin, crispy, and cooked on a hot griddle, typically served hot with sambar and coconut chutney. This staple food is naturally gluten-free and known for its fermented, slightly tangy flavor.

Biriyani

Biriyani is a fragrant, celebratory rice dish from South Asia, combining long-grain basmati rice with marinated meat (chicken, mutton, beef), vegetables, or seafood. It is defined by layers of spices, caramelized onions, herbs, and saffron, often cooked together using the dum pukht (slow-steamed) method to blend flavors.
